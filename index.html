Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to TapATale ("we," "our," or "us"). We create safe, engaging, and imaginative interactive storybook experiences for children and families. Protecting the privacy of our young users and their families is our highest priority.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection, usage, and disclosure when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Kids Category & Family Commitment: TapATale is built strictly for family audiences. We do not harvest personal data, do not profile children, and run zero third-party behavioral advertisements.

1. Compliance with Children’s Privacy Laws (COPPA & GDPR-K)

TapATale is built for early childhood audiences. We strictly comply with:

  • The Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States.
  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K) in the European Union.
  • Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines for the Kids Category.

We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure complete safety and privacy for children, TapATale:

  • Does not collect names, home addresses, emails, phone numbers, or dates of birth from children.
  • Does not access or track precise GPS location data.
  • Does not use device microphones, cameras, or photo libraries for data collection.
  • Does not create behavioral profiles or track children across third-party apps or websites.
  • Does not integrate social media networks or public chat/messaging features.

3. Information We May Process

The App operates with minimal data processing, purely to support internal operations:

  • Technical Diagnostics: Aggregated, anonymous crash reports and device performance metrics (such as OS version and device model) solely to resolve bugs and optimize stability.
  • Local Device Storage: Reading progress, unlocked stories, and sound settings are saved locally on your device or linked to your private Apple iCloud account (if enabled). This data remains under your direct control.
  • In-App Purchase Receipts: Transactions are handled securely through Apple’s StoreKit framework. We never access, store, or process your credit card numbers or payment details.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising

TapATale contains no third-party behavioral, contextual, or targeted advertising. We do not embed ad-tracking SDKs that monitor or harvest data from children.

5. Parental Gates & External Links

Any interaction outside the reading experience—such as purchasing additional stories, managing settings, contacting support, or visiting external links—is protected behind a secure Parental Gate designed to prevent accidental interactions or purchases by children.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not store personal accounts or child information on remote servers, there is no remote personal profile data to store. You can erase all locally stored story progress and cached data at any time simply by deleting the App from your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our App or changes in legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your child’s privacy, please reach out to:
